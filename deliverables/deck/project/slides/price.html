  • The June file’s price field is a quote for one stay near the scrape date, mostly the week of 15 June, with later quotes in match weeks.
  • The same listings cost 16% more in June than in September, and up to 37% more in match weeks.
  • Decision 1: divide June quotes by 1.14. Core one-bedroom yield falls from 3.0% to 2.3%.
  • Winter prices run 12% below September, so a flat annual price would overstate the off-season.
