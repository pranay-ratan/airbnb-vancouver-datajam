0:15–1:05. Fourteen one-bedroom condos: nine Downtown, four in Gastown and Chinatown, one in the West End. We expect $270K of net operating income in 2027, and in eight simulated years out of ten it lands between $213K and $340K. That is a 2.8% return on all-in cost. Our metric is 2027 net operating income divided by everything it costs to get a unit running: price, BC transfer tax, legal and furnishing. We chose it over revenue per dollar because property tax, strata and upkeep rise with price, and that is exactly what sinks houses. It is all cash, income only, with no price growth assumed.
