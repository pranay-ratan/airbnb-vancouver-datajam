3:55–4:35. This is the biggest risk. A city can ask the Province to opt out of the principal-residence rule if its rental vacancy has been three percent or more for two years. Kelowna did on 1 June 2026. A Vancouver request by 28 February 2027 could take effect on 1 June, five months into our measured year. The map shows where that supply sits: the share of entire homes with a ninety-night minimum, which is outside the short-term definition today. In the core there are 657 such apartments against 1,254 on the nightly market. If every one switched and demand stayed flat, nightly revenue per listing would fall 34 percent. Our two-calendar plan holds $232K at a twenty percent fall, while nightly-only drops to $153K. That is the main reason for the furnished months.
