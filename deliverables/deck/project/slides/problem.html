THE PROBLEM

DDD has $10M and three open questions.

A fund wants income from BC short-term rental property in 2027. One public snapshot of the market, and rules that change what any building is worth.

Where

Which market, and which neighbourhoods inside it. Vancouver has 6,509 listings and they do not earn alike.

How many, and what kind

A few houses or many condos. The answer changes the risk more than it changes the headline revenue.

How to run them

Nightly, a 12-month lease, or both. The City and the Province tax the choice.

Vancouver: principal-residence only, licence and provincial registration required

Empty six months: Empty Homes Tax 3% + Speculation and Vacancy Tax 1%
