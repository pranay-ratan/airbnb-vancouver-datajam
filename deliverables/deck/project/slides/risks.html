5:05–5:50. Three risks. First, the opt-out. At ten, twenty and thirty-four percent less nightly revenue, income is $250K, $232K and $202K. The mitigation is built in: eight months of the year are furnished stays outside the nightly market. Second, paying too much. Twenty percent above benchmark takes the yield from 2.9% to 2.3%; we set an offer ceiling per building and ask each seller for twelve months of actual bookings, because occupancy here is an estimate. Third, our tax reading. Counsel signs off before the first offer. Two more, briefly: strata bylaws that ban short stays, and capital value, since apartment prices fell five point nine percent in the year to October. Our yield counts income only.
