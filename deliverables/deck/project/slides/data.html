The question, the data and what we kept

  • Question: where should $10M earn the most net income in 2027, and what could break the plan?
  • Data: Inside Airbnb, Vancouver, 15 June 2026: 6,509 listings, a year of calendar, 323,903 reviews.
  • Also used: the March and September snapshots, Greater Vancouver REALTORS benchmark prices, CMHC and liv.rent rents, City and Province tax rules.
  • Limits: prices are one day’s quotes; occupancy and revenue are Inside Airbnb estimates; licence status is an estimate.
