5:50–6:25. We also tell you what did not hold. Superhosts show 168 estimated nights against 132, but that estimate is just reviews times six, and the forward calendar shows no gap, so we left that premium out. A one-night minimum looked better, but the calendar showed no clean pattern, so we did not rely on it. Five nightly months, two-bedrooms and a house with a suite and laneway home all lost to the plan. And what held up: re-running everything on the September snapshot gives 2.2% against 2.3%; we reproduced Inside Airbnb's own occupancy formula for 100% of listings; and the top segments stayed on top in 99% of three hundred random splits.
