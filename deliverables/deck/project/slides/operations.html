  • Nightly beats a lease for only 45% of comparable one-bedrooms.
  • A home not rented for 6 months in stays of 30+ nights owes the City’s Empty Homes Tax (3%) and the Province’s Speculation and Vacancy Tax (1%): about $26K a unit.
  • Decision 5: nightly May-August (49% of a year’s stays), furnished 30+-night stays the other eight months.
  • A fifth nightly month leaves 3.9 of 14 units short of the six-month test, so we did not take it.
