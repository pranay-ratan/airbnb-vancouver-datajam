3:10–3:55. Decision five. Letting by the night all year earns about $220K, roughly what a plain twelve-month lease earns, $228K. Worse, a home that is not rented for six months in stays of thirty nights or more owes the City's Empty Homes Tax of three percent and the Province's Speculation and Vacancy Tax of one percent of assessed value. Nightly-only would turn into a loss of about $152K. Half of a year's stays fall in May to August, so we let by the night then, and furnished for thirty nights or more the rest of the year: $270K. We tested a fifth nightly month; it leaves 3.9 of fourteen units short of the six-month test. That tax reading is ours, and we want counsel to confirm it before any offer.
